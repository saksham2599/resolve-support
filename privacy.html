Your privacy.

This policy describes Resolve, developed by Saksham Goyal, and this support website.

Information on your iPhone

Routines, schedules, fixed obligations, focus history, completion records, notes, personal points, awards, rewards, and reminder settings are stored locally. Resolve does not send these detailed records to Apple Game Center or a Resolve server. Your device’s backup settings may include app data in a device backup.

Optional Apple Game Center leaderboard

League is optional and free. When you connect Game Center, Apple authenticates your Game Center player. Resolve uses a game-scoped player identifier to keep your participation associated with the correct Apple account. Only after you separately consent to score sharing and join a weekly round does Resolve submit your aggregate weekly score to Apple Game Center. It does not submit routine names, your schedule, notes, individual completion records, or focus durations.

Apple stores and processes leaderboard scores and player information. Your Game Center name, score, and rank may be visible to other players under Apple’s Game Center settings and policies. Choosing Friends in Resolve filters the standings you view; it does not make your submitted score private. Resolve reads player names, scores, and ranks to show standings and keeps this display cache in memory. Resolve has no separate online account or leaderboard server. See Apple Game Center & Privacy.

Purchases and permissions

Apple processes purchases. Resolve checks Apple’s verified subscription entitlements to provide Pro access; it does not receive payment-card information. Notification, alarm, and Screen Time permissions are optional and can be managed through iOS. Opaque app-selection tokens for distraction protection remain on the device.

No advertising or tracking

Resolve contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics integrations. We do not sell your app data or use routine records for advertising.

Your choices and deletion

Export my records creates a file containing your private routine details. You control where it is saved or shared. Stop sharing in League prevents future score submissions from this installation and removes local league enrollment. Delete my data clears local app records, stops score sharing, and cancels scheduled reminders, alarms, and protection. These actions do not erase scores already held by Apple Game Center. Manage Game Center through your Apple Account settings and contact Apple regarding data it holds. Deleting app data or uninstalling Resolve does not cancel an Apple subscription.

This website and support

This website uses GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts or cookies added by Resolve. GitHub may process technical request data under its own privacy statement. Support issues are hosted publicly on GitHub. Do not include private app records, credentials, or purchase receipts in a public issue.

Policy changes and contact

The effective date above identifies this policy. Material changes to data use will be reflected here and, when appropriate, in the app before they apply. For questions about this policy, use the support page.
