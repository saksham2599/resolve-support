Your practice.
Your privacy.

This policy describes Resolve, developed by Saksham Goyal, and this support website.

Information on your iPhone

Routines, schedules, fixed obligations, focus history, completion records, notes, personal points, awards, rewards, and reminder settings are stored locally. Resolve does not send these detailed records to its circle service. Your device’s backup settings may include app data in a device backup.

Optional private circles

If you choose an available circle service and accept its sharing notice, it receives an opaque account identifier, your chosen alias and circle name, circle membership, consent version, weekly aggregate score, and the timestamps needed to manage participation. Circle members can see aliases, totals, ranks, and membership. These are pseudonymous records associated with a circle account, not anonymous data. Your alias and score may reveal participation.

Circle access and invitation codes are stored as hashes by the reference service. The access key is stored in this iPhone’s Keychain. This version has no cross-device account-recovery flow. Do not include private routine details in an alias or circle name. The current beta can run without an online circle endpoint; in that case no circle account is created or transmitted.

Purchases and permissions

Apple processes purchases. Resolve checks Apple’s verified subscription entitlements to provide Pro access; it does not receive payment-card information. Notification, alarm, and Screen Time permissions are optional and can be managed through iOS. Opaque app-selection tokens for distraction protection remain on the device.

No advertising or tracking

Resolve contains no advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics integrations. We do not sell your app data or use routine records for advertising.

Your choices and deletion

Export my records creates a file containing your private routine details. You control where it is saved or shared. Leaving a circle removes its membership and associated scores. Delete my data removes the connected circle account, memberships, and scores before clearing local app records and scheduled reminders, alarms, and protection. If a network deletion fails, Resolve retains local records and explains that you can retry. Uninstalling alone does not request deletion of an online circle account. Deleting data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

This website and support

This website uses GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts or cookies added by Resolve. GitHub may process technical request data under its own privacy statement. Support issues are hosted publicly on GitHub. Do not include private app records, credentials, or purchase receipts in a public issue.

Policy changes and contact

The effective date above identifies this policy. Material changes to data use will be reflected here and, when appropriate, in the app before they apply. For questions about this policy, use the support page.
