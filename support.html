Resolve is an iPhone app by Saksham Goyal. This is the official support page for its current beta and upcoming App Store release.

How do I start?

Open Resolve, finish onboarding, and choose a commitment in Today. Use Routines to change its schedule, duration, or minimum version.

What if I miss a routine?

Choose the commitment that needs a new plan. Move it to an available time, use its minimum version, or choose intentional rest. Completed records remain in your history.

Why did a reminder or alarm not arrive?

Check Settings → Reminders & alarms inside Resolve and the system permissions in iPhone Settings. Resolve schedules a bounded set of upcoming reminders when you open it. Start alarms require iOS 26 or later and separate permission. Keep essential alarms independently available.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Use Restore purchases on the Resolve Pro screen with the Apple Account used to buy it. Manage or cancel renewals in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Removing the app or deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Resolve Settings → Your data. Export my records saves a private JSON file. Delete my data clears local records, stops future Game Center score sharing, and cancels scheduled attention settings. Scores already held by Apple Game Center are not deleted by this action. Export and deletion remain free.

How does the weekly Game Center league work?

Open League, connect Apple Game Center, and separately choose whether to share scores. Pick three routine anchors and five days before joining the next Monday-to-Monday UTC round. A full completion earns two points and a minimum completion earns one, up to thirty points a week. The plan stays on your phone. Game Center receives only your aggregate score.

New participation begins next Monday at 00:00 UTC; it cannot add a score to the current round. Open Resolve after completing your routines so it can synchronize during the round. You need internet access and Game Center enabled on your Apple Account. Friends filters the list you see and does not hide your own score from other players. Stop sharing prevents future submissions; it does not erase Apple’s existing scores.

Report a problem.

Include your iOS version, Resolve version, and the steps that caused the issue. GitHub issues are public: do not post routine details, receipts, phone numbers, or other private information.
